Hi, I'm Lucas, a computer science student and aspiring software developer with an interest in backend development, databases, and building practical software solutions.

My journey in technology has been driven by curiosity and a desire to understand how things work. I enjoy taking a problem, breaking it down into smaller pieces, and figuring out how to turn an idea into something functional. Through my Computer Science degree, I've developed experience with Python, Java, JavaScript, databases, APIs, and web development. I've also learned that good software development involves more than writing code. It requires thoughtful design, testing, problem-solving, and an understanding of the people who will use the final product.

Outside the classroom, I've gained experience working with business operations and technology, from supporting software systems to configuring networks and managing technical projects. That experience has influenced how I approach development. I value solutions that are practical, maintainable, and designed around actual needs rather than unnecessary complexity.

I'm particularly interested in backend development, software architecture, and database systems. I enjoy learning new technologies, improving existing applications, and understanding the decisions that make software reliable and secure. I also believe that learning doesn't stop when a project is finished. There is always something to improve, a different approach to consider, or a new skill to develop.

This portfolio brings together projects that reflect my growth throughout my Computer Science program. Each project represents an opportunity to apply what I've learned, solve problems, and improve my approach to software development. My goal is to continue growing as a developer, contribute to meaningful projects, and build software that makes a practical difference.
